Our Story

Guppy Guide began with a single ten-gallon tank and a lot of trial and error. What started as one hobbyist's notes on water parameters, strain lines, and fry survival rates grew into a place where guppy keepers of every level could stop guessing and start breeding with confidence.

Today, Guppy Guide is built by people who still change their own water every week — not a team that outgrew the hobby. Every guide, tracker, and tip on this site comes from a tank we're actually running.
